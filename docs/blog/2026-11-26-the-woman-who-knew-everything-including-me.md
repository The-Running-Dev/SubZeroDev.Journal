# The Woman Who Knew Everything, Including Me

*Lucifer, Discount Store Marcus Aurelius, and a three-hour international tribunal that somehow started on Tinder.*

I met a woman on Tinder.

She seemed interesting. Intelligent. Opinionated. A little older than me.

We started talking.

Three fucking hours later, we had apparently debated nationality, Bulgarian society, artificial intelligence, the nature of human identity, Russian science fiction, Jules Verne, personal experience, and the philosophical foundations of objective reality.

At some point, my own life became evidence in a trial I hadn't agreed to attend.

And here's the most ridiculous part.

**I liked her.**

<!-- truncate -->

I genuinely fucking liked her.

Apparently, my idea of an interesting conversation is one in which two people can disagree about practically everything without requiring diplomatic intervention.

Unfortunately, that particular diplomatic mission eventually collapsed.

## The Ministry of Bulgarian Identity

She was born in Moscow and had moved to Bulgaria.

I was born in Bulgaria, left when I was seventeen, and have lived much of my life elsewhere.

Somehow, this became a problem requiring immediate clarification.

I explained that although I was born here, I don't consider myself Bulgarian.

That should have been the end of it.

Apparently not.

She insisted that I was Bulgarian.

Because I was born here.

Wonderful.

A stranger had just appointed herself the Minister of My Personal Identity.

Somewhere in my imagination, Lucifer leaned forward.

"Brother, this is extraordinary. You've spent an entire lifetime being yourself, and this woman has resolved your identity in a single conversation."

"Apparently."

"Think of the time you could have saved!"

And it got better.

She wanted my opinion of Bulgaria.

I warned her.

I explicitly warned her that she probably wouldn't like what I had to say.

But she kept pressing.

And pressing.

Eventually, I gave her exactly what she'd asked for.

My opinion.

Based on my experiences.

And suddenly, we had a national emergency.

She began defending Bulgaria as though I'd personally invaded the country.

Here's the beautiful contradiction.

I was Bulgarian enough for her to insist on defining me that way, but apparently not Bulgarian enough to criticize the fucking country.

Lucifer nearly applauded.

"Darling, you've been granted citizenship without the right to complain. How terribly efficient."

## The Department of Artificial Intelligence

Then we reached technology.

Now, I've spent about twenty-five years working in software.

I've worked with enough systems, technologies, architectures, and engineering problems to know a few things.

And these days I'm doing considerable work with AI.

Does that make me an authority on everything?

Of course fucking not.

It does, however, give me rather more than a passing acquaintance with the subject.

But apparently we needed another hearing.

She challenged my understanding of AI, and I found myself in the peculiar position of having my professional knowledge dismissed during a Tinder conversation.

Lucifer was delighted.

"Brother, perhaps you should send her your résumé."

"Why?"

"So she can correct it."

"I've been doing this for twenty-five years."

"Yes, but have you considered that she may have an opinion?"

"EVERYBODY HAS A FUCKING OPINION!"

"Precisely. And apparently hers comes with administrator privileges."

## The Russian Science Fiction Department

Eventually, we arrived at human nature.

She believed some things about people never change.

I disagreed.

Not necessarily with the possibility that certain characteristics can remain stable.

I disagreed with the insistence that her view was an absolute truth about everybody.

People can change. Beliefs can change. Identity can change. Experiences can change our understanding of ourselves.

Maybe some things remain constant. Maybe others don't.

What the fuck do I know?

That's the point.

But she seemed determined to establish a universal rule.

Then came a Russian science-fiction author whose work she admired.

I don't remember enough of that exchange to reconstruct his name or argument, but she regarded his ideas as having been vindicated by reality.

My response?

**"Well, I've read Jules Verne. And everything Jules Verne wrote about is true too. So fucking what?"**

I wasn't suggesting that every sentence Jules Verne ever wrote was literally factual.

I was pointing out that speculative fiction sometimes anticipates reality.

That's interesting.

It doesn't automatically make an author the supreme authority on existence.

Lucifer considered the matter.

"So we've established that science fiction can contain astonishing predictions."

"Yes."

"And therefore one should accept an author's entire understanding of humanity?"

"NO!"

"Wonderful. I was beginning to worry that logic had left the building."

## And Then She Crossed the Line

Up to this point, I was enjoying myself.

Yes, really.

I like a debate.

I can argue passionately about a subject and still respect somebody who disagrees with me.

But then we discussed my experience of being assaulted at a hospital.

And she suggested that perhaps it had been my fault.

She began making excuses for the hospital staff.

Now, that was different.

She wasn't debating some abstract philosophical proposition.

She was questioning an experience I had actually lived through, while apparently possessing none of the information necessary to override my account.

I told her she'd crossed a line.

And I became very assertive.

For something like ten minutes, I challenged the arguments she'd been making.

I wasn't yelling, despite how heated the exchange became. But I certainly wasn't softening my position to make the conversation comfortable.

Eventually, she went quiet.

I asked why.

The response was that it was too much.

Too much?

My dear woman.

You repeatedly demanded my opinion after I warned you that you wouldn't like it.

You insisted on defining my identity.

You challenged my experiences.

You questioned my expertise.

And after all that, the intensity of my response was suddenly the problem?

**Madam, you ordered the fucking meal. I merely served it.**

## Discount Marcus Enters the Ruins

At this point, Discount Store Marcus Aurelius appeared in my imagination.

He'd been purchased on clearance and was therefore missing some of the philosophical accessories.

"One must remain calm in the presence of disagreement."

"I WAS CALM, MARCUS."

"One must respect differing opinions."

"I DID!"

"One must recognize that others experience the world differently."

"THAT WAS MY ENTIRE FUCKING ARGUMENT!"

Lucifer leaned toward him.

"Marcus, I believe our brother has spent three hours defending the right of two people to disagree."

"An admirable undertaking."

"And the other participant appears to have mistaken disagreement for an invitation to establish a universal dictatorship of opinion."

Marcus considered this.

"Perhaps both could have disengaged sooner."

I looked at him.

"Marcus, please return yourself to Lidl."

## The Punchline

Now here's what makes the whole thing hilarious.

**I still liked her.**

All those debates hadn't made her uninteresting to me.

Quite the opposite.

I was still open to going out with her.

I don't require someone to agree with everything I think.

Hell, I don't even agree with everything I thought five years ago.

But I do require one distinction.

Your opinion is yours.

My experience is mine.

We can question ideas, debate conclusions, discover errors, and change our minds.

What we can't reasonably do is appoint ourselves the ultimate authority over somebody else's entire existence.

When the conversation finally ended, I said goodbye.

Lucifer raised his glass.

"Well, brother, after three hours of international diplomacy, technical peer review, literary criticism, and a personal identity tribunal, what have we learned?"

"That I still liked the woman."

He stared at me.

Then burst out laughing.

"Of course you fucking did."

Discount Marcus sighed.

"Perhaps next time, a quieter conversation."

"Marcus, it was Tinder. Not the fucking United Nations."

Lucifer smiled.

"Brother, at least the United Nations has an agenda."

**FOR THE GLORY OF ROME!**
